TL;DR: no change needed, WriteSerializable is fine.

- For write conflicts, WriteSerializable only differs from Serializable on blind appends (an INSERT that doesn't read the table). That's what the quoted sentence is about.
- The lock never does a blind append. Take/release are UPDATEs and adding the row is a MERGE, and both read the row first.
- Two UPDATEs/MERGEs on the same row at the same time conflict under both levels. The second job gets ConcurrentAppend/DeleteRead/DeleteDeleteException, which we catch, then re-read and wait.
- Row-level concurrency (what can "resolve" UPDATEs instead of rejecting them) only works for different rows, and partitioned tables don't use it anyway.
